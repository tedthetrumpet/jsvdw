## 06/12/2015 for pelog gamelan

A sort of sampak/kebyar fusion piece, pulling together some of the livelier ideas from the Javanese traditions with a Balinese-inspired melody. Performed by Gamelan Naga Mas on a number of occasions.

Also exists in a version for flute, trombone, violin and percussion.

Duration ~2'
