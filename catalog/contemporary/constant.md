## 02/04/2025 for viola and piano

### About

An older meaning of ‘constant’, as in a constant friend: reliable, dependable.

### To the pianist

You are the constant one here: never getting faster, always steady, perfectly smooth when a note changes across the barline, only the slightest of dynamic shadings.

###  To the violist

There is some freedom in the way the part floats across the piano accompaniment: some of the notated rhythms are a bit fiddly and can be treated more in the nature of a notated rubato. Try to get to the next bar on time though, particulary where there is a new note on the downbeat rather than a tie from the previous bar.

(Bowings in this first draft are my best guess: please as you see fit.)
