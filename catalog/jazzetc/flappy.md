## 10/07/25 for jazz big band

Continuing a theme of writing for the [Byres Road Big Band](https://byresroadbigband.wordpress.com/), this jazz waltz features baritone sax and trumpet somewhat the manner of Gerry Mulligan and Chet Baker.

I wrote this mainly on the guitar, and the harmony is kind of… not that jazzy. I keep thinking of Bacharachs' 'South American Getaway' from Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid, although, apart from the time signature, it's really not very much like that at all.
