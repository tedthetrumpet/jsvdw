## 10/01/96 (rev 2025) for jazz big band

This piece was written (and perhaps recorded??) for the Tartan Amoebas album 'Evolution', but never released. The title comes from 'The Big Sleep' by Raymond Chandler.

The big band version was scored for the [Byres Road Big Band](https://byresroadbigband.wordpress.com/) and tried out on a couple of occasions, but not actually gigged yet, I think.
