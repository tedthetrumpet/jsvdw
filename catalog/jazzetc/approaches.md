## 01/4/97 (rev 2025) for jazz big band

The original version of this piece was created many years ago during an 'Approaches to Composition' weekend run by [More Music in Morecambe](https://moremusic.org.uk/), and scored for female voice, flugelhorn, clarinet, alto sax, tenor sax (optional), guitar, bass, djembe.

Around 2001 I rewrote it for Invention Ensemble, and I believe we may have played it on a number of occasions.

This latest version for big band has been workshopped by the Mackintosh Jazz Composers Ensemble, and is slated for performance by the [Byres Road Big Band](https://byresroadbigband.wordpress.com/) at some point.
