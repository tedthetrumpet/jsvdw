## An invitation

### A collaboration with [Mags Smith](https://www.glimmering.scot/)

1. **Light Heart**
2. **Raining Bells**
3. **Tree Song**
4. **Crosscurrents**
5. **Hanging In There**
6. **Big Tree**
7. **Time and Tide**

These seven music videos were made during the Covid-19 restrictions when Good Vibrations could not run workshops with participants. They were an attempt to bridge the gap: to reach over the gulf of restrictions and interact creatively when we couldn't be there in person.

>‘The thing I miss most about being in a good vibrations workshop is interacting in the music… responding… playing in the moment, bouncing off an idea from someone else, discussion and insight.’
 
Each piece was looking for a response, an interaction – whether musical, visual art, words, or dance. We were not asking for people to critique the music or even like the music but to answer with a piece of art of their own, to invite interaction, creativity and expression – a bit like sending a sound out into deep space to see if there is anyone else out there.

### Suggestions on how to use

* Watch the video and notice your first response…
    * Love it / hate it? 
    * Bored/curious? 
    * Movement/stillness 
    * Toe tapping / tension / swaying? 
    * Do words come to mind?
* Watch a few times
    * Play or sing along
    * Move or dance
    * Sketch or draw
    * Write words, poetry
* Consider creating and sharing something with us
    * Film/record or photograph anything you make while listening/watching the video
    * Create something new of your own

### Behind the scenes

We started with the small set of gamelan instruments we have at home and improvised on the gender and gambang on four or five different days for an hour or so listening back to see if we had played any sounds/ textures/ideas we wanted to repeat and make into pieces.

Light Heart and Tree Song came straight out as pieces from that process and were recorded live.  Other pieces we recorded and then “layered up” other musical instruments on top in digital audio workstation. Some of the pieces were processed using delay, reverb, and so on. Crosscurrents includes some material coded in SuperCollider.

All the videos were taken by Mags on a mobile phone, some as far back as 2011 and simply pasted together in a movie editor, no special effects
