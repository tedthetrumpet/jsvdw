So… I've changed the title and programme note for the violin and piano piece being done in St Andrews next week.

It's now called '0utburst' instead of 'p01ntless'. Which is much better – why would someone want to play or listen to a piece of music that the composer thought was pointless!

Also changed the programme note to just two sentences, instead of the reams of nonsense I wrote before. It just says:

> I wrote this piece on a day I was feeling really down.
>
>I feel better now.

Baichuan and Inês have just run the piece through for me: their performance was perfect, they are superb musicians. Just for once, I'm genuinely 'really looking forward' to the performance of a new piece!
