In May 2026 I’m going to be 65 years old :) and I’m planning a couple of events around that time to celebrate and enjoy some of the music I’ve written over the decades.

One of the most important events I’m looking forward to is a concert with the [Byres Road Big Band](https://byresroadbigband.wordpress.com/) (date tbc). I’ve been lead trombone with them for two and a bit years now, greatly enjoying playing my second instrument just for fun.

I’ve written [several](/catalog/jazzetc/bigbig) [original](/catalog/jazzetc/flappy) [charts](/catalog/jazzetc/isthiscool) for them, sometimes (but not always) rearrangements of [older compositions](/catalog/jazzetc/cahuenga) of mine. The major ambition for the concert in May is to perform ‘[Between Two Oceans](/catalog/jazzetc/oceans)’, a suite of three jazz pieces originally written in in 1998.

I tried out some of this material last year with the [Mackintosh Jazz Composers Ensemble](https://www.instagram.com/mackintoshjazzcomposers/), and am now in the process of rescoring it for the BRBB. I’ve decided to make the whole thing a guitar feature for our excellent young guitarist Matthew, who has recently started studying at the RCS.

Here’s the probably-nearly-finished BRBB version of the third movement of the suite ‘Ninety Mile Beach’:
